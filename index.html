Calls and offline use

Offline access keeps this page and these tools available after the first visit. Offline transcription works only in browsers with a supported on-device speech model.

Carrier real-time text (RTT) and automatic speaker identification are not exposed to this browser app. Use your phone's built-in RTT/accessibility settings for calls. Speaker labels below are manual.
